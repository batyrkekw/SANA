A cartoon comic page: a strict 2 by 2 grid of four equal square panels, thin black borders. Read left to right, top to bottom. Same student, clothes, glass and window in all panels. Expressive faces, clean colorful ink drawing. No lettering or speech balloons. Each panel follows its own scene description:
Top left panel: Proud schoolgirl, black bob, yellow hoodie, raises a finger beside a tall glass on a windowsill. Three small ice cubes lie at the bottom of the glass. No liquid.
Top right panel: The same girl looks shocked at the same glass. Two small melting ice cubes rest in a tiny puddle inside the glass. The glass stays on the windowsill.
Bottom left panel: The same girl points at the sunny window with a knowing smile. In the glass only one tiny ice chip floats in shallow water at the bottom.
Bottom right panel: The same girl smiles and facepalms beside the glass. The glass contains only a little transparent water, filling its bottom fifth. Absolutely no ice, no cubes, no solid objects.
